Why the change: the prover's sphincs mode prints its key and signature as bare hex. The stub prints
them with 0x, and AG-11 item 1 reads a triple's key as 0x plus 64 hex digits, so on the real binary no
key was foldable and round 1 stopped with "a node's prover made no fold". The prover's aggregate
mode takes either form. The harness change prefixes 0x where the test reads the sphincs answer:
